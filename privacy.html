CHASSIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy covers the CHASSIS game for Android, Windows and the web demo.

What we collect

Nothing. CHASSIS does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your game progress (fighters, levels, settings and in-game currency) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Internet access

The Android app declares the standard internet permission because it runs in a web view. The game itself loads everything from inside the app and does not send any data over the network.

TikTok LIVE (Windows only)

The Windows version has an optional TikTok LIVE mode. If you turn it on and enter a TikTok username, the game connects to that public live stream to read public events (comments, likes, gifts and viewer names) and show them in the game. This data is used only while the mode is running and is not stored or sent anywhere else. This mode is not part of the Android app.

Children

CHASSIS is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
